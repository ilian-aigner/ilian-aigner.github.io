The starting point was classroom entrepreneurship work. We explored an initial fitness problem through interviews, business model canvases, and research synthesis. Some of those conversations were about an earlier concept, before the current STRK direction existed.

My documented contributions include interview work, synthesizing what we heard, developing business model canvases, and helping shape positioning and product direction. I contributed to a **team discovery effort spanning 80+ customer interviews**. That is a team total, not a personal interview count.

The direction evolved toward an end-user fitness coaching experience. The proposed product helps someone define goals, follow a personalized plan, record activity and feedback, and adapt over time. AI is intended for everyday guidance. A connected human trainer is optional and may provide contextual support, review, and changes to future plans.

STRK is still being developed and tested. Historical research helped us identify questions, but it does **not** demonstrate that the present product is validated, launched, clinically effective, or proven to save trainers time. Current work is about testing the experience with trainers and clients and learning what actually helps.
