### Start in the classroom

STRK began in an entrepreneurship class with an earlier fitness concept. We took it beyond the classroom: talking to potential customers, working through business model canvases, and comparing what people told us with what we had assumed.

### Work through the evidence

I contributed to the team’s customer discovery effort. My work included interview synthesis, business model canvases, positioning, and helping shape the next product direction. Those conversations gave us sharper questions and a reason to keep building.

### Change the direction

The idea evolved into an end-user fitness coaching app. We are developing and testing an experience around goals, personalized plans, activity, and feedback, with AI for everyday guidance and optional connected human trainer support.
